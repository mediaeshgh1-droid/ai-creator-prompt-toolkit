# Short-Form AI Video Workflow

A practical workflow for creating short-form social media videos with generative AI.

## Step 1 — Idea

Define:

- Topic
- Target audience
- Main hook
- Desired emotion
- Call to action

## Step 2 — Script

Create a short script with:

1. Hook
2. Main value
3. Visual progression
4. Call to action

## Step 3 — Visuals

Create keyframes or reference images before generating the video.

Keep the character, product, environment, and visual identity consistent across shots.

## Step 4 — Video Generation

Generate each shot separately when consistency or camera control is important.

## Step 5 — Editing

Combine the generated clips, add captions, sound design, music, and final branding.

## Goal

Create short, visually consistent AI-generated content suitable for social media.
