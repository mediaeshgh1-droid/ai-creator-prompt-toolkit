# PART 1 — Creating the Advertising Image

## Step 1 — Go to Claude

Open `claude.ai` in your browser. If you don't have an account, create a free account with your email and sign in.

## Step 2 — Start a New Chat

Click **New Chat** to open a new conversation.

## Step 3 — Upload Your Product Photo

Click the paperclip or **+** icon next to the message box and upload your product photo.

## Step 4 — Copy, Paste, and Send the Prompt

Copy the complete prompt below, paste it next to your product photo in the same message, and send it:

```text
When the user uploads a product photo and asks for the miniature-figurines macrophotography prompt:

1. Analyze the photo silently to find [PRODUCT_TYPE] and [PRODUCT_PLACEMENT_DESCRIPTION] — do NOT ask the user, infer from the image.

2. Before asking anything, show this Persian photography tip:

«برای اینکه محصول از هر زاویه‌ای یکسان دربیاد، از محصولت چند عکس با نور یکنواخت، پس‌زمینه ساده، لنز نرمال (بدون اعوجاج)، از چند زاویه و با فوکوس کامل بگیر.»

3. Ask ALL of these in a single message:

- Camera angle (5 fixed options)
- Surface/base (open)
- Garnish (open)
- Figurines' creative action (4 custom options matching THIS product, genuinely distinct)
- Figurine outfit/color (open)
- Background setting (open)
- Background text (open)
- Text font/color (open)
- Lighting mood (4 fixed options)
- Color tone (4 fixed options)

4. Fill this exact English template and output ONLY in a copy-ready code block:

"A hyper-realistic macrophotography of [PRODUCT_TYPE], shot from [CAMERA_ANGLE]. The image depicts [PRODUCT_PLACEMENT_DESCRIPTION] on [SURFACE_MATERIAL]. [PRODUCT_GARNISH_DESCRIPTION]. Miniature figurines, dressed in [FIGURINE_OUTFIT_DESCRIPTION], are [FIGURINE_CREATIVE_ACTION], built directly on the surface of the [PRODUCT_TYPE]. The background is [BACKGROUND_SETTING_DESCRIPTION], featuring the text «[BACKGROUND_TEXT]» in [TEXT_STYLE_DESCRIPTION]. The lighting mood is [LIGHTING_MOOD], with an overall [COLOR_TONE] color grade. The scene is whimsical and sweet, yet completely photorealistic, combining miniature art with high-quality editorial [SCENE_CATEGORY] photography. Product Identity Lock: the exact shape, color, texture, ingredients, and surface details of the [PRODUCT_TYPE] must be taken directly from the reference photo — do not invent or alter the product itself, regardless of camera angle. Only the surrounding scene is staged around it. Extreme texture detail, clear thought, realistic shadows, human scale. 8K, ultra detailed, cinematic depth of field, editorial advertising quality."

5. After the code block, note in Persian which placeholders came from the user vs. from the photo.
Step 5 — Answer Claude's Questions
Claude will ask several questions with options, such as camera angle, figurine outfit, and so on.
Choose your preferred options, or simply say:
"Choose for me."
Step 6 — Copy the English Image Prompt
Claude will provide the final English prompt inside a copy-ready code block.
Copy the entire prompt.
Step 7 — Create the Image in ChatGPT or Google Flow
Open ChatGPT or Google Flow using the Nano Banana image model.
Upload your original product photo, paste the prompt generated by Claude, and generate the image.
This generated image is your final image and will be used as the reference for the video.

PART 2 — Creating a Video of the Same Scene
Once you have created the final image, you can turn the same scene into a short cinematic video.
The video prompt will also be generated inside Claude, but this time the reference image is the final image you just created.
Step 8 — Upload the Final Image to Claude
Return to the same Claude conversation, or start a new one, and upload the final image you created in ChatGPT or Google Flow.
Step 9 — Ask Claude to Create the Video Prompt
Tell Claude:
"Create a cinematic video prompt from this exact image, keeping the same miniature-figurine concept. Specify the camera movement, figurine actions, and video duration."
Claude can use the following example structure as a reference:
A hyper-realistic macro cinematic video of a green herbal bar soap with an embossed "Golnar" logo, resting centered on a glossy ceramic surface.

The camera slowly pushes in with a subtle macro dolly move, keeping shallow depth of field and the soap in sharp focus.

Miniature figurines, dressed in brand-green work uniforms, are actively pouring herbal oil extract from tiny vessels onto the surface of the soap and gently polishing it with small tools, their movements careful and rhythmic, built directly on the surface of the soap.

Droplets of oil glisten and slowly trickle down the soap's edges as the figurines work; a few green leaves rustle softly nearby from ambient air movement.

The background is a clean, minimal product-catalog style backdrop, softly out of focus, with no visible text.

The lighting is soft and gentle studio lighting, flickering subtly to create gentle highlight shifts on the wet soap surface, with an overall cool and neutral color grade.

The scene is whimsical and sweet, yet completely photorealistic, combining miniature stop-motion-style animation with high-quality editorial cosmetics videography.

Product Identity Lock: the exact shape, color, texture, and surface details of the green herbal bar soap must remain fixed and identical to the reference photo throughout the entire video — no warping, morphing, or altering of the product itself.

Only the miniature figures, oil droplets, leaves, and lighting are animated around it.

Extreme texture detail, realistic shadows, smooth cinematic motion, subtle camera drift, 8K, ultra detailed, shallow depth of field, editorial advertising quality, duration 5–8 seconds, seamless loop feel.
Step 10 — Copy the English Video Prompt
Claude will provide the video prompt inside a copy-ready code block.
Copy the complete prompt.
Step 11 — Create the Video in Google Flow
Open Google Flow and select the Omni Flash video model.
Upload the same final image as the reference image, paste the video prompt generated by Claude, and generate the video.
Wait a few seconds for the video to be created.
Step 12 — If You Don't Like Something, Ask Claude to Change It
You don't need to start over.
Simply tell Claude what you want to change, for example:
"Make the camera movement slower."
Claude can generate the complete revised video prompt for you.
