# Simple Guide to Creating Product Advertising Images with AI — 9 Separate Prompts

hajimohtava.ai

────────────────────────────────────────

This guide is designed for anyone who has never worked with AI tools before. Each slide contains one step or one prompt. Just follow the steps in order.

════════════════════════════════════════

## What is this post about?

════════════════════════════════════════

With AI and just one simple photo of your product, you can create 9 professional advertising images. Each image has a different concept, while your product remains exactly the same product in all of them.

────────────────────────────────────────

## What do you need?

────────────────────────────────────────

1. A clear photo of your product (you can take it with your phone; just make sure the lighting is good and the background is not cluttered).

2. One of these two free tools: ChatGPT or Gemini.

### How to upload:

Open ChatGPT or Gemini, click the paperclip or + icon next to the message box, and upload your product photo.

────────────────────────────────────────

## How do these prompts work?

────────────────────────────────────────

Each prompt below is an English text that you need to copy. Each one contains several words inside `[ ]`, such as `[PRODUCT_NAME]`. Replace them with information specific to your product. An example is provided next to each one.

**Important:** All 9 prompts must include your product photo. Every time you send a new prompt, attach the product photo again.

Before generating the image, simply send the prompt exactly as written below together with your product photo to ChatGPT or Gemini and say:

**"Adapt this to my product."**

The AI will analyze your product photo and identify its color, shape, material, and other characteristics, then use that information to fill in the appropriate placeholders.

# Prompt 1: Main Product Image (Hero Shot)

════════════════════════════════════════

This prompt creates a simple and elegant image of your product, similar to the type of photography you see in luxury store displays.

### Placeholders you need to fill in:

- `[PRODUCT_NAME]` → Your product name (example: `Rosewood Matte Lipstick`)
- `[SURFACE_MATERIAL]` → The surface the product should sit on (example: `white marble`)
- `[BRAND_PRIMARY_COLOR]` → Your brand's primary color (example: `dark red`)
- `[BRAND_SECONDARY_COLOR]` → Your brand's secondary color (example: `gold`)

### Prompt:

```text
Create a single high-end commercial product photograph, 3:4 aspect ratio.

The product shown in the uploaded reference image is [PRODUCT_NAME]. Use this exact reference for shape, proportions, label text, typography, logo placement, color values, cap/lid design, and material finish. Do not alter, redesign, restyle, or reinterpret the product. It must look photographed, not illustrated or stylized.

Lighting: single soft key light from upper-left at roughly 45°, fill light at 20% intensity from the right, no colored gels, 5600K neutral daylight balance, soft shadow falling to the lower-right.

Composition: product centered, three-quarter angle, resting on a [SURFACE_MATERIAL] surface, single soft shadow beneath, negative space above for potential headline placement. Sharp focus on the entire product, background softly out of focus. Background palette derived from [BRAND_PRIMARY_COLOR] and [BRAND_SECONDARY_COLOR].

Technical spec: 8K resolution equivalent detail, ultra-sharp focus, high dynamic range, no distortion of proportions, clean product-to-background edge separation, no color bleed/halo.
````

# Prompt 3: Dynamic Material Motion Around the Product

════════════════════════════════════════

This prompt shows something dynamic moving around your product — such as droplets, colored particles, or powder — frozen in mid-air.

### Placeholders you need to fill in:

* `[PRODUCT_NAME]` → Your product name
* `[BRAND_PRIMARY_COLOR]` → Your brand's primary color
* `[BRAND_SECONDARY_COLOR]` → Your brand's secondary color

This prompt has no other placeholders — the AI will determine the appropriate material to use based on your product photo.

### Prompt:

```text
Create a single high-end commercial product photograph, 3:4 aspect ratio.

The product shown in the uploaded reference image is [PRODUCT_NAME]. Use this exact reference for shape, proportions, label text, typography, logo placement, color values, cap/lid design, and material finish. Do not alter, redesign, restyle, or reinterpret the product.

Lighting: single soft key light from upper-left at roughly 45°, fill light at 20% intensity from the right, no colored gels, 5600K neutral daylight balance.

Composition: identify the product's own core material or defining sensory trait from the reference image (e.g. if liquid-based: the liquid itself; if solid/dry: fine particles matching its actual texture — powder, dust, fragments, droplets of condensation, or shavings — whatever is physically native to that specific product, not an unrelated substance).

Show that native material captured mid-motion in a frozen-action moment — either erupting/splashing upward around the product, orbiting it in a dynamic radial burst, or cascading past it — using a shutter-speed-frozen look (crisp edges on every droplet/particle, zero motion blur, like a high-speed strobe capture).

Material color must be pulled directly from the product's own real-world color or ingredient (not an arbitrary contrasting color).

Product itself stays perfectly still, sharp, and physically untouched by the motion — the energy exists only in the surrounding material, never deforming or splashing onto the product's readable label/branding area.

Product placed slightly off-center (rule-of-thirds), material interaction fills the opposite third of the frame for dynamic tension. Background palette derived from [BRAND_PRIMARY_COLOR] and [BRAND_SECONDARY_COLOR].

Technical spec: 8K resolution equivalent detail, ultra-sharp focus on product, high dynamic range, no motion blur on the product itself, no distortion, clean edge separation.
```

# Prompt 7: Product-Matched Monochromatic Scene

════════════════════════════════════════

This prompt creates a monochromatic background based on the color of your product — while keeping the actual product colors completely unchanged.

### Placeholders you need to fill in:

* `[PRODUCT_NAME]` → Your product name
* `[DOMINANT_PRODUCT_COLOR]` → The dominant color of the product itself, not its packaging (example: `deep red`)

**Note:** This prompt does not require brand colors because the entire background is built around the product's own color.

### Prompt:

```text
Create a single high-end commercial product photograph, 3:4 aspect ratio.

The product shown in the uploaded reference image is [PRODUCT_NAME]. Use this exact reference for shape, proportions, label text, typography, logo placement, color values, cap/lid design, and material finish. Do not alter, redesign, restyle, or reinterpret the product.

CRITICAL EXCEPTION: the monochromatic color rule below applies ONLY to the background/environment — every element that is NOT the product itself. The product's actual label colors, cap color, and branding colors must remain 100% identical to the reference image, completely unaffected by the scene's color wash. The product keeps its true original colors even while surrounded by a monochromatic environment.

Lighting: single soft key light from upper-left at roughly 45°, fill light at 20% intensity from the right, no colored gels, 5600K neutral daylight balance.

Composition: full-frame color wash derived directly from [DOMINANT_PRODUCT_COLOR], product placed off-center within a monochromatic environment of that same hue family, creating a tonal, editorial-color-story frame.

Contrast rule: the background must be a lower-saturation, lower-contrast tint/shade of [DOMINANT_PRODUCT_COLOR], while the product itself keeps its full saturation and true value — separation comes from value and saturation contrast within the same hue family, never from introducing a different hue.

Technical spec: 8K resolution equivalent detail, ultra-sharp focus, high dynamic range, no distortion of proportions, clean edge separation.
```
