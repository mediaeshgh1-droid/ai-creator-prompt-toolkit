# Image Generation Prompts

A collection of practical image-generation prompt patterns for content creators.

## Reference Image Preservation

Use the uploaded reference image as the primary source of truth.

Preserve the subject's identity, facial structure, skin tone, hair, clothing, proportions, and important visual details.

Do not redesign, beautify, or randomly reinterpret the subject.

## Product Advertising

Use the uploaded product reference as the exact source of truth.

Preserve the product's shape, logo, colors, materials, proportions, and branding.

Do not redesign or invent product details.

## Notes

These prompts are templates and should be adapted to the specific image-generation model and use case.
